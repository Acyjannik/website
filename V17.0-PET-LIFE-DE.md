# ACYJANNIK V17.0 — ACY PET LIFE

V17 turns the companion into a small game loop.

## New
- 5 care actions with daily limits
- slower pet decay; pets no longer die from inactivity
- pet inventory and food
- AC Coins (provisional club currency)
- daily supply
- Mystery Box
- Pet Shop
- Snack Hunt and Lucky Paw mini-games
- wheel/daily/quest systems remain separate and compatible

## Deploy
1. Deploy V17.0.
2. Run `supabase/v17.0-pet-life.sql` once in the Supabase SQL Editor.
3. Hard refresh/PWA update.

## Important
AC Coins are an internal, non-purchasable prototype currency in V17.0. No real-money purchase system is introduced.
