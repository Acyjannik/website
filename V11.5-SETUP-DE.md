ACY V11.5 — Wheel & Notifications

1. Run in Supabase:
   - `supabase/v11.5_wheel_notifications.sql`
   - `supabase/v11.5_notifications_cleanup.sql`

2. Deploy V11.5.

Wheel:
- The current state is loaded immediately after the Club profile opens.
- If a spin happened in the previous 24 hours and there is no Extra-Dreh token, the button is disabled immediately and shows the remaining cooldown.
- 12 outcomes are displayed and backed by the database.
- New rewards: +500 XP, +1,000 XP, Pet-Mega-Boost, 2 Extra-Drehs, +2,500 XP Jackpot.

Notifications:
- Individual notifications can be deleted with `×`.
- "Alle löschen" deletes the full notification history for the current account.
- Deleted notifications are no longer returned by the notification API, so they do not come back on reload.
